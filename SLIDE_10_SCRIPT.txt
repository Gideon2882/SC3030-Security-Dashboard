Slide 10 — Hands-On Experiment: LLM Network Security Validation

For my hands-on experiment, I built a Python Streamlit dashboard to validate LLM-generated Cisco access-control lists, using five security checks and three operational checks. Both original configurations passed four out of four checks, but only five out of eight when assessed against the expanded policies. With all eight requirements specified, both new configurations passed eight out of eight. This highlights the importance of complete policy coverage and independent validation, rather than an advantage of either prompting method. The source code, configurations, and reproducible tests are on my GitHub. The tool uses a limited static ACL model, not live network testing.
